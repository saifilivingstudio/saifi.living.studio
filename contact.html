Before you message us

Do you charge for the site visit?

No. The visit and the measurement are free, and you get a written quotation afterwards.

Can I start with just one wardrobe or one room?

Yes. Many clients start with a single wardrobe, a kitchen or one ceiling. You can add more later.

Do you work outside East Delhi?

Yes. We work across Delhi and NCR. Tell us your locality when you message and we will confirm how we plan the visit.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Your locality, a few photos or a short video of the space, and what you want built. A rough size helps, but it is not needed to start.

What happens after you message us.

We call or WhatsApp you back to fix a time, come and measure, and then send the quotation in writing. The visit itself is free.
