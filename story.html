How it started in 1991

Mehmud Ali has spent thirty-five years as an interior contractor in Delhi. He learned the trade on site, not in an office, and he has taken homes and workspaces from the first measurement to the last coat of polish more times than he can count.

Over those years he built a network of karigars he trusts: carpenters, ceiling fixers, painters and polish men who know how he wants a job finished.

What changed

The next generation now runs the business side. That means a written, itemised quotation before work starts, a date you can plan around, and photos from site while the work is going on.

It also means you can find us, message us and see our work before you call, which is why this website exists.

What hasn't

He still checks every joint before a handover. Shutters must close true, edges must be clean, ceilings must read level under light. If something is not right, it is redone before you see it.
