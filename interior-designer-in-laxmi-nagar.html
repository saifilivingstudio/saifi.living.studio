Laxmi Nagar mixes homes and business. Residential lanes are mostly builder floors and rented flats, while the main roads carry shops, coaching centres and small offices. Homes need durable kitchens and storage that suit a busy family; commercial spaces need work finished quickly so the business can open again.

Planning interior work in a Laxmi Nagar home

Rented flat or your own home?

For a flat that is rented out, most owners want work that lasts through several tenants: laminate that wipes clean, strong hinges and paint that is easy to touch up. In your own home you can choose finishes for looks first. Tell us which it is and we will suggest materials to match.

A ceiling that suits a shop

PVC panel ceilings are quick to fix, easy to wipe down and stand up to road dust, so we often suggest them for shops and coaching rooms. Homes usually get gypsum, which gives a smoother finish under cove lights.

Builder floors: mark the kitchen points first

In many builder floors the sink, drain and gas point were fixed by the builder. We mark them before planning the kitchen, so the new units work with them, or we tell you clearly what needs to move.

Questions from Laxmi Nagar

Can you finish a shop interior without long closures?

We plan commercial work around your hours and can schedule noisy work for when the shop is closed. Tell us your constraints at the site visit.

Do you work on rented flats?

Yes. Owners often ask us to refresh a flat between tenants with paint, polish and repairs, or to add a kitchen and wardrobes.

How do I get a quotation?

Message us on WhatsApp with your address. We visit, measure and send an itemised quotation.

Do you take on offices and showrooms too?

Yes. Alongside homes, we fit out shops, showrooms, coaching rooms and offices: cabins, workstations, storage and ceilings.

Are you an interior designer or a contractor?

Both. We plan the layout, materials and finishes with you, and our own karigars build and fit everything. If you already have an architect or designer, we execute their drawings.
