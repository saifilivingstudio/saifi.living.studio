Modular kitchens for East Delhi homes, measured on site.

A kitchen is used every day, several times a day. We plan it around how your family cooks, then build it from boards and hardware that keep working for years.

Ask for a quoteBook a free site visit

Measured first, then built

Before we quote, we measure every wall, window and plumbing point on site. Old buildings in East Delhi are rarely square, and a kitchen planned from rough measurements is where most problems begin. Your quotation lists the material, finish and hardware for every unit, so you can compare it fairly.

Layouts we build

  • Straight kitchens for compact flats
  • L-shaped and U-shaped kitchens
  • Parallel kitchens for long, narrow rooms
  • Breakfast counters and islands where space allows

Finishes and hardware

  • Laminate, acrylic and PU shutters
  • Moisture-resistant boards near the sink
  • Soft-close hinges and channels
  • Baskets, tall pantry units and lofts

Questions about modular kitchens

How is the cost of a modular kitchen worked out?

It depends on the size, the material and the hardware. After measuring, we give an itemised quotation so you can see what each part costs.

Can you renovate an existing kitchen?

Yes. We can replace shutters and storage on the existing platform or rebuild the kitchen completely.

Do you install the chimney and hob?

We plan the cut-outs and space for them. Tell us the models at the visit so the units fit exactly.
