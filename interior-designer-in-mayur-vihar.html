Homes in Mayur Vihar

DDA flats have fixed walls and smaller kitchens, so the gains come from planning: corner units, tall pantry storage and wardrobes that use the full height of the room. In societies, work hours, lift use and debris removal are usually controlled by the RWA, and we plan the job around those rules from the start.

Planning interior work in a Mayur Vihar home

Measure the lift and the stairs

Before tall wardrobe panels or a sofa are made, we check the lift size and the turn of the stairs, so large pieces are made in sections that fit.

Small kitchens: use the corners

DDA kitchens are narrow, so corner units, a carousel, a tall pantry and a loft over the platform add more storage than wider cabinets would.

Ground floor or top floor?

Ground-floor flats can have damp near the floor, and top floors heat up under the roof. Both change which boards and finishes we suggest, so mention your floor when you message us.

Questions from Mayur Vihar

Will you follow our society's working rules?

Yes. Share the RWA rules at the site visit and we schedule noisy work, deliveries and debris removal around them.

Can you redo an old DDA kitchen without breaking walls?

Usually yes. Most kitchen upgrades in DDA flats are done on the existing walls with new platforms, shutters and storage.

Do you cover all three phases?

Yes, we work across Mayur Vihar Phase 1, 2 and 3.

Can you work while we stay in the flat?

Yes, if the work is planned room by room. We cover your furniture, clean up at the end of each day and agree the working hours with you.

Are you an interior designer or a contractor?

Both. We plan the layout, materials and finishes with you, and our own karigars build and fit everything. If you already have an architect or designer, we execute their drawings.
