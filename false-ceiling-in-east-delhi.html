Questions about false ceilings

Which is better: gypsum, POP or PVC?

Gypsum is quick and clean, POP allows more shapes, and PVC suits damp areas and shops. We recommend one for each room at the visit.

Can you add a ceiling in a rented or older flat?

Yes, as long as the existing ceiling is sound. We check this at the visit.

Do you do the electrical work for lights?

We plan the light positions with you and coordinate with the electrician so wiring is ready before boarding.
