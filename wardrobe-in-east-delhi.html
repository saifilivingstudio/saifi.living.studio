Wardrobes built to the exact size of your walls.

A good wardrobe uses the full height of the room and is planned inside around what you actually store. Ours are measured on site and made by our own carpenters.

Ask for a quoteBook a free site visit

Measured first, then built

We match the finish across every shutter so the grain and colour run on, and we check that every door closes true before handover. If a wall is out of line, we correct for it in the frame rather than leaving a gap.

Types

  • Sliding wardrobes for tight rooms
  • Hinged wardrobes with lofts
  • Walk-in and open wardrobes
  • Wardrobes with dressers, mirrors or a mandir

Inside

  • Hanging space at the right heights
  • Drawers, trouser racks and lockers
  • Loft storage for seasonal items
  • Lighting inside shutters where wanted

What decides the cost

Two wardrobes of the same size can cost very different amounts, depending on the board, the finish and the hardware. That is why we quote only after measuring, and why your quotation lists each part separately.

Priced separately in your quotation

  • Size: the full width and height, including any loft
  • Shutter type: hinged or sliding
  • Finish: laminate, acrylic, veneer or marble-finish laminate
  • Inside fittings: drawers, lockers, trouser racks and lights
  • Extras such as a dresser, a mirror or a mandir

Questions about wardrobes

Sliding or hinged: which is better?

Sliding shutters save space in front of the wardrobe; hinged shutters cost less and give full access. We advise based on your room at the visit.

Can you make a wardrobe with a built-in mandir?

Yes. You can see a wardrobe wall with a mandir in the centre on our work page.

How long does a wardrobe take?

It depends on the size and finish. The schedule is written into your quotation.

Should a wardrobe go up to the ceiling?

In most rooms, yes. A loft or a full-height unit adds space for suitcases and bedding without taking more floor.
